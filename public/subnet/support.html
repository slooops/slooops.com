CONTACT

Found a bug, stuck on a sector, or have an idea? Email jacobsloop@gmail.com. Including your device model and iOS version helps.

HOW DO I PLAY?

Open BRIEFING from the main menu. It walks through every mechanic with live diagrams of the real board.

WHERE IS MY PROGRESS SAVED?

On your device only. There are no accounts, so deleting the app also deletes your campaign progress and settings.

DOES IT NEED AN INTERNET CONNECTION?

No. SUBNET runs entirely offline.
