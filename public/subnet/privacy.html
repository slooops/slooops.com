SUBNET

Privacy Policy · Effective October 5, 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

SUBNET does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The game never connects to the internet.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

The game saves your settings (difficulty, board style, push ratio, hints, haptics) and your campaign progress locally on your device. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to the developer or anyone else, and is removed when you delete the app.

THIRD PARTIES

SUBNET contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising services.

CHILDREN

Because no personal information is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

CHANGES

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
