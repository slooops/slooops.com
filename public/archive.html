During my time at the Univeristy of St Andrews, I produced thousands of pages of magazine spread, across 3 major student publications. Here's a selection of my favorite spreads from my time at College Echoes, The Gay Saint, and Hearing Aid Magazine for St Andrews Radio.

I've poured myself into these pages-from queer student zines to campus satire, crafting layouts, covers, and brand identities that speak with beauty and care.
