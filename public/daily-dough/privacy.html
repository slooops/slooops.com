Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version: Daily Dough uses your bank data to work out what you can spend each day, and for nothing else. We don't sell your data or show ads, and there's no analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • What you enter: your paycheck amount and schedule, rent, bills and ignore rules.
  • What your bank shares through Plaid when you connect an account: account names and types, the last digits of account numbers, balances, and transactions.
  • What the app works out from those: daily allowances, your slush fund and streaks.

How bank connections work

Daily Dough connects to your bank through Plaid. You sign in through Plaid or your bank, so Daily Dough never sees your bank username or password. Plaid handles your information under the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. Daily Dough can read your account data but can't move money.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored on Daily Dough's server so it can stay in sync with your bank. The access token for each bank connection is encrypted when stored.

Who we share it with

Only Plaid, and the hosting providers that run Daily Dough's server, as needed to operate the app. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting your accounts in the app ends Daily Dough's access through Plaid and deletes the connected accounts and their transactions. To delete everything else, including your budget settings, email us.

Children

Daily Dough isn't intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
