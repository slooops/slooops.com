Help with Daily Dough

Email jacobsloop@gmail.com with questions, bugs or ideas. Including your device model and iOS version helps.

How is my daily number calculated?

Daily Dough takes your take-home pay, subtracts your rent and bills, and spreads what's left evenly across each day. Money you don't spend carries forward, and overspending comes out of the days after it.

Why isn't a transaction counted?

Paychecks, transfers between accounts, credit card payments, rent and bills you've added are left out, because they're already part of your daily number. You can also ignore a transaction or merchant yourself.

Why don't I see my latest purchase?

Banks can take a while to report new transactions. Open the app again a little later.

My bank stopped updating

Banks sometimes ask you to sign in again, and some connections expire after a set time. Reconnect the account in Settings.

Is Daily Dough financial advice?

No. Daily Dough is a budgeting tool, and its numbers are estimates. Check your bank for your actual balances.
