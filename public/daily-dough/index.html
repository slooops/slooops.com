Daily Dough

In testing  For iPhone

Skip the monthly budget. Daily Dough turns your paycheck and bills into one number: what you can spend today.

What it does

  • A daily dial showing what's left to spend today.
  • Connects to your bank through Plaid, so spending shows up on its own.
  • Leaves out paychecks, transfers, card payments and bills you've already planned for.
  • Rolls unspent money forward into your slush fund.
  • Tracks your streak of days within budget.
