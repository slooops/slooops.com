Moonbeam

Coming soon  For iPhone and iPad

Sleep smarter, not longer. Moonbeam plans your night in whole sleep cycles, so your alarm goes off at the end of one instead of halfway through.

What it does

  • A circular sleep dial that snaps to cycle boundaries. Drag bedtime or wake-up and the other end follows.
  • Real wake-up alarms that ring through Silent mode.
  • Sunrise and sunset drawn right on the dial.
  • Jet lag plans that shift your sleep toward a destination's time zone, day by day.
  • Tune your own cycle length and how long you take to fall asleep.

What it doesn't do

No accounts, no subscription, no ads, no tracking. Your sleep settings stay on your device.
