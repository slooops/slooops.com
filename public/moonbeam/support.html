Help with Moonbeam

Email jacobsloop@gmail.com with questions, bugs or ideas. Including your device model and iOS version helps.

My alarm didn't go off

Check that Moonbeam is allowed to schedule alarms in Settings → Moonbeam. Moonbeam's alarms ring through Silent mode and Focus, but they need that permission to be scheduled.

How do I cancel an alarm?

Open Moonbeam and cancel it from the app. Deleting the app also removes its alarms.

Why are my sunrise and sunset times wrong?

Moonbeam uses your location to look them up. If location access is off, it falls back to 6:30 AM and 8:00 PM. Turn it on in Settings → Moonbeam → Location.

What's a sleep cycle?

Sleep runs in cycles of roughly 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels better than waking in the middle of one. Everyone's a little different, so you can set your own cycle length and fall-asleep time in Moonbeam's sleep profile.

Is Moonbeam medical advice?

No. Moonbeam is a planning tool, not a medical device. Talk to a doctor about sleep problems.
