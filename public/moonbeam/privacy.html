Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

The short version: Moonbeam doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your settings, trips and alarms stay on your device. To show sunrise and sunset times, Moonbeam looks them up with a free online service using a location, and that's the only thing that leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Your sleep profile (cycle length, fall-asleep time, ideal bedtime and wake-up), your jet lag trips, and your scheduled alarms are stored only on your device. They're never sent to us or anyone else, and they're removed when you delete the app.

Location and sunrise times

If you allow location access, Moonbeam asks iOS for your approximate location so it can show sunrise and sunset on the sleep dial. It rounds those coordinates to about 1 km and sends them to sunrise-sunset.org, a free public service, which replies with that day's sun times. Moonbeam doesn't store your location, and we never receive it. The service may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, under its own policies.

Location access is optional. Without it, Moonbeam uses default sun times.

Jet lag plans

When you type a city that isn't in Moonbeam's built-in airport list, Moonbeam uses Apple's geocoding service to find it, then looks up that city's sun times as described above. Apple handles those requests under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Alarms and notifications

Alarms and reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. Moonbeam doesn't use push notifications and has no server.

Children

Moonbeam doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
