Support

Help with Pebbles

Email jacobsloop@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. Including your username, iPhone model and iOS version helps.

Report a problem or abuse

In the upcoming profile-and-safety beta, open a person’s profile from Friends or a chat and tap Report user. You can also long-press an incoming message and choose Report message, or tap the flag while viewing a photo or video. Choose a reason and optionally describe what happened. Message text is shared for review only if you opt in; media is not copied into reports.

Jack reviews reports privately in the beta’s safety queue. For follow-up, email jacobsloop@gmail.com with your report reference. Reports expire after 30 days and are removed by scheduled cleanup.

Remove or block someone

Open their profile from Friends or a chat. Remove friend stops new sends until you accept a new friend request, while existing history and saved copies remain. Block user stops new sends and requests in both directions and hides the conversation. Manage your block list from your own profile; unblocking does not restore the friendship. None of these actions can erase someone else’s saved copy or screenshot.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send a reset link. Open it on the same iPhone that requested it to choose a new password in the app. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

I never got my confirmation email

Check spam first. If it's still missing, try signing in. Pebbles will tell you if the email still needs confirming. Still stuck? Email us from the address you signed up with.

Why can't I send to someone?

You can only send to accepted friends. Search their username in Friends, send a request, and wait for them to accept.

Where did my pebble go?

Photo and video pebbles have one viewing session per recipient, currently up to two minutes. Server access expires seven days after upload preparation, and cleanup can remove media earlier after all recipients finish. Text expires after 24 hours. To keep a pebble, save it while viewing; this lets both participants retain independent copies on their phones. Server deletion runs asynchronously.

Delete my account

In the upcoming profile-and-safety beta, open Friends → profile icon → Delete account, read the confirmation and enter your current password. This deletes the account and associated hosted app data, plus the vault on this iPhone. Others’ saved copies/screenshots cannot be remotely erased; reports submitted by others follow the safety-retention policy. Keep the app open and retry if cleanup fails. If you cannot sign in, email us from your account’s email address for help. These controls require the updated app and backend rollout; until then, contact support for help with deletion.
