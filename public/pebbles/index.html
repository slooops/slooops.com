Pebbles

In beta  For iPhone

A small, private way to share everyday moments with your friends. Snap a photo or a short video, add a note, and send it to the people you choose.

How it works

  • Add friends by username. Both of you have to accept before anything can be sent.
  • Photo and video pebbles open for one viewing session. Server access expires within a week, with earlier cleanup after everyone finishes.
  • Save the ones you love to a private vault on your phone.
  • Text replies keep the conversation going and expire after a day.

What it doesn't do

  • No feed, no follower counts, no public profiles.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.
  • No phone number or contacts upload. Just an email to sign in.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Pebbles is in private beta via TestFlight.
