Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 · Pebbles is in beta, and this policy will be updated as the app changes

The short version: Pebbles collects only what it needs to let you send photos, videos and messages to friends you've chosen. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your data for advertising. Media is removed from our servers when it expires or after viewing finishes. Saved copies can remain on both the sender’s and recipient’s phones.

Who we are

Pebbles is made by Jack Sloop, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the Pebbles team. Questions go to jacobsloop@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), plus the username and display name you choose. Your username and display name are visible to people who search for you or are your friends.
  • Connections: friend requests, accepted friendships, and your blocked-user list.
  • Safety reports: the account and optional message identifiers, reason and description you submit. A copy of message text is included only if you explicitly choose it; photos and videos are not copied into reports.
  • Messages and media: the photos, videos, notes and text messages you send, along with who sent them, who received them, and when they were sent, opened and saved.
  • Push notification tokens: if you allow notifications, a device token from Apple so we can tell you when a friend sends you something.

We don't ask for your phone number or contacts, and we don't collect your location, advertising identifier or browsing activity.

Camera, microphone and photos

Pebbles asks for camera access to take photos and videos, and microphone access to record sound in videos. If you choose a picture from your library, only that picture is used. Nothing is captured or uploaded unless you send it.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, including confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • To deliver what you send to the friends you choose, and nobody else.
  • To send push notifications. These show the sender's username and whether it's a photo, video or message, never the content itself.
  • To respond to support requests and reports of abuse, and to keep Pebbles safe.

How long we keep it

  • Photos and videos: each recipient can open a pebble for one viewing session, currently limited to two minutes. Media access expires seven days after upload preparation; sends inherit that deadline. Cleanup can remove media earlier after all recipients have finished viewing, saved it, or their viewing windows have ended. Physical deletion runs asynchronously and retries if needed.
  • Text messages: access expires 24 hours after sending. Scheduled cleanup removes the server records.
  • Saved pebbles: the sender temporarily keeps a hidden local copy while waiting for the recipient’s decision. If the recipient saves, both participants can retain independent copies in their phones’ private vaults. Otherwise, the sender’s copy is pruned when the app next reconciles the decision or expiry. The server keeps a save receipt (participants, note and timestamps); saving does not keep the media on the server indefinitely.
  • Safety reports: reports expire after 30 days and are removed by scheduled cleanup. They are accessible only to the Pebbles team for safety review. If a reported account is deleted, its account reference is removed; evidence explicitly submitted by somebody else can remain until report cleanup. Reports you submitted are deleted with your account.
  • Account details and friendships are kept until you delete your account.
  • Push tokens are removed when you sign out, when Apple reports them invalid, or when your account is deleted. Tokens inactive for 90 days are excluded from delivery; the registration record may remain until one of those removal events.

Copies on devices

Expiry applies to server access. Both participants can retain a saved pebble in the app. A recipient can also screenshot it or record their screen, and we can't delete those copies. Only send things to people you trust. Pebbles' private vault is stored with iOS data protection and is excluded from iCloud and device backups, so it is removed when the app is deleted. You can remove a saved copy from this iPhone using the message’s menu; that does not remove another person’s copy.

Messages are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on access-controlled servers. Pebbles does not currently offer end-to-end encryption.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers to run Pebbles, who process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and accounts, and sends account emails.
  • Apple delivers push notifications and distributes the app (App Store and TestFlight).

We may disclose information if required by law, or if it's necessary to protect someone's safety.

Deleting your account

In current app builds, open Friends, tap the profile icon, and choose Delete account. Confirm the consequences and enter your current password. Deletion removes your Auth account, profile, friendships, blocks, hosted messages, owned uploaded media, saved-pebble records and push tokens. The current iPhone’s vault and session are cleared after successful deletion. If cleanup fails, your account may be paused; retry from the same profile screen. Copies already saved on other people’s devices and offline copies on your other devices cannot be remotely erased. If you can't access the app, email us from your account’s email address for help verifying your identity and completing deletion.

Deletion concerns active app data. Hosting-provider backups and security logs may have separate retention and cannot be promised to disappear at the same instant. Contact us for questions about the provider’s current retention practices. Updating from an older beta may be required to see the new account and safety controls.

Blocking and reporting

Open a person’s profile from Friends or a chat to remove a friend, block them or report them. Blocking stops new messages and requests in both directions and hides the conversation; unblocking does not restore the friendship. Alerts already dispatched may still arrive. Existing saved copies are not remotely erased. Long-press an incoming message or tap the flag while viewing a pebble to report it. Jack reviews the private report queue; email us with the report reference for follow-up.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help. You can change your username in the app at any time.

Children

Pebbles is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

We'll post any changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.
